03 · When to go

The best time to visit

Turkey is great all year round — just pick the right region for the season.

Spring

April–May: blossoms and mild weather, ideal for sightseeing.

Best for cities

Summer

June–August: hot, warm sea. Peak prices and crowds.

For the beach

Autumn

September–October: golden season, the sea is still warm.

Best value for weather

Winter

November–March: few tourists, low prices, skiing at Uludağ.

For budget trips

07 · Practical

Useful tips

Small things that make the trip easier.

🛂

Documents

Entry rules depend on your citizenship — check the visa regime on the official website before buying tickets.

💳

Money

The currency is the Turkish lira. Cards work almost everywhere, but carry some cash for markets and taxis.

🚌

Transport

Get an Istanbulkart in Istanbul; for long distances use comfortable intercity buses or domestic flights.

🕌

Etiquette

Cover shoulders and knees in mosques and remove your shoes. Polite bargaining is normal in markets.

📱

Connectivity

An eSIM or a local SIM card from the airport is the easiest option.
